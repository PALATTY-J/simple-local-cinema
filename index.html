Your own little cinema

Install Local Cinema for a dedicated app window and offline playback. Your videos stay on your device.

Use your browser’s Install app option. On iPhone or iPad, open Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Mac, Safari offers File → Add to Dock. Options depend on your browser.

Open the app online once and wait for “Ready for offline use” before going offline. Choose your video again each time you reopen.
